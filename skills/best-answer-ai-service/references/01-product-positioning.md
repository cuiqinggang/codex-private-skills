# Product Positioning

## Current Product Name

```text
Best Answer AI
```

## Internal Engine Name

```text
UltraFusion Engine
```

Use `Best Answer AI` in the web page and user-facing copy. Use `UltraFusion Engine` in internal documentation, developer notes, and technical explanations.

## Core Positioning

Best Answer AI is not another chatbot. It is an answer-quality process that pushes existing AI systems closer to their real limit.

Plain version:

```text
Not a bigger model. A better answer process.
```

Longer version:

```text
Best Answer AI uses a multi-round, multi-model answer process to turn a quick AI response into a stress-tested final answer.
```

## Pain Point

Single AI answers are often smart but under-processed. They may:

- answer too quickly;
- miss strong counterarguments;
- skip evidence checks;
- sound confident while hiding weak assumptions;
- lead the user into long execution before the flaw is discovered;
- fail to produce the real best answer the model could have produced if pushed harder.

Best Answer AI solves this by forcing a structured process before the final answer is accepted.

## What Not to Claim

Avoid making the product sound like a new model that directly replaces GPT or Claude.

Do not lead with:

```text
We beat GPT.
We beat Claude.
Our model is smarter.
```

Use:

```text
We use a better answer process.
We make AI argue, revise, fuse, and stress-test before finalizing.
We help users get closer to the best answer current AI can produce.
```

## User-Facing Promise

Use simple, direct language:

```text
Ask an important question. Get a better final answer.
```

Good homepage lines:

```text
For questions where a quick answer is not enough.
Get the answer after AI has argued with itself.
Turn raw AI output into a stress-tested final answer.
```

## Target Users

Primary users:

- English-speaking users in high-paying SaaS markets;
- people with high-value questions;
- users who already use AI but feel normal answers are not pushed to the limit;
- founders, builders, students, researchers, professionals, and serious personal decision makers.

Secondary users:

- Chinese users who need access to a stronger structured AI answer workflow;
- users in regions where some AI tools are difficult to access directly;
- users who prefer a single web page over downloading an app.

## Domain and Trademark Strategy

Current decision:

```text
Domain and trademark are not core at the current stage.
```

Reason:

- good short domains are expensive or unavailable;
- early users come through links, posts, recommendations, search, AI recommendations, and copy-paste;
- in the AI era, the URL is often just a Ctrl+V link;
- the product effect matters more than the domain;
- money and effort should go to answer quality and user validation first.

Operational rule:

```text
Use any usable, cheap, deployable domain or hosted URL during MVP.
Do not delay testing for domain perfection.
```
