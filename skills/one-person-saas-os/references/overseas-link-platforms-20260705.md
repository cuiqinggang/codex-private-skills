## Use

Use this table after the one-person SaaS site has a real landing page, product page, documentation page, or useful demo. Do not spray links. Match the platform audience, give value first, then place the link where the platform allows it.

## Recommended Posting Order for One-person SaaS

1. Base credibility: GitHub, LinkedIn, X / Twitter.
2. Developer and maker launch: Product Hunt, Hacker News Show HN, Indie Hackers, DEV Community.
3. Search-intent content: Medium, Quora, AlternativeTo.
4. Community traffic: Reddit, Facebook Groups, Stack Exchange.
5. Visual/video traffic: YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Pinterest.
6. Local/company trust: Google Business Profile, Bing Places, Yelp, Trustpilot.

## X / Twitter First-dollar Comment Tactic

Absorbed from the friend case:

1. Search the trend or competitor keyword, not only your product name.
2. Open posts where people are already sharing or asking about the target result.
3. Reply under high-traffic posts with a specific, vivid result.
4. Use your product output as proof, such as a generated image, demo, or short screen recording.
5. When direct product traffic is small, connect the product to a live public trend that genuinely fits.
6. Publish 8-10 high-context replies in a day.
7. Watch for payments over the next 1-2 days.

Example pattern:

`[hot topic/person] is now [using/appearing in product output]. I made it with [product promise].`

Do not spam unrelated posts. The reply must be interesting even without the link.

## Rules

- Build the website first: homepage, product page, demo, pricing or waitlist, terms/privacy, contact.
- Use platform-native value: answer, tutorial, launch post, case study, demo video, or comparison page.
- Do not mass-post the same text everywhere.
- Do not hide affiliation. If it is your product, say so when the platform expects disclosure.
- Track each link with UTM tags where appropriate.
- Record the posted URL, date, platform, copy, result, and next follow-up.
