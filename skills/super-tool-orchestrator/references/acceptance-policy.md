# Acceptance Policy

L4-L5 tasks require final acceptance artifacts.

## Required Files

```text
final-acceptance.md
final-acceptance.json
```

## Final Decision Values

- `PASS`
- `PARTIAL`
- `FAIL`
- `BLOCKED`

## PASS Requires

- original goal addressed,
- non-goals respected,
- evidence listed,
- verification passed,
- safety boundaries checked,
- skipped risks disclosed,
- handoff included,
- evolution candidates separated from completion claims.

Do not use elapsed time, amount of work, or GPT confidence as proof of completion.
