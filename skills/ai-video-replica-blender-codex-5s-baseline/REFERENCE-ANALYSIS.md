# Reference Analysis

## Source Characteristics

- Approximate source duration: 23.456790 seconds.
- Source resolution: 1080x1260.
- Source frame rate: about 29.97fps.
- Style: dark desktop or notebook ritual scene with red magical energy.

## Core Visual Elements

- Dark desk or table surface.
- Notebook or dark background plane.
- Red glowing orb.
- Magic circle with rings, triangle/star geometry, symbols, and red emission.
- Candles or warm vertical light sources.
- Central dark stone or black metal object.
- Small debris, particles, or rock fragments.
- Red glow and subtle camera motion.

## Minimum 5 Second Read

The first validation should establish the core silhouette and motion rather than chase full detail. A red orb over a glowing circle with candles and debris is enough to prove the closed loop.

## Difficulty

The hard parts are not modeling; they are repeatability, reliable headless Blender execution, media validation, and keeping the scope from expanding into full 23 second production.

## Replica Judgment

This baseline is suitable for process validation and P2 visual iteration. It is not suitable as a final reference-quality replica.
