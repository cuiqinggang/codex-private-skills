# Shot Structure Checklist

## Establish, 0-5s

- Wider framing.
- Smaller or less intense energy orb.
- Table, background panel, candles, circle, and central object are readable.

## Intensify, 5-10s

- Camera moves closer.
- Red light and orb scale increase.
- Magic circle motion and glow become more active.
- Debris or energy particles show more motion.

## Climax, 10-15s

- Strongest red energy read.
- Closest camera composition.
- Magic circle remains visible.
- Candles or warm light remain present as context.
- Shot ends with a resolved high-energy frame.

## Scope Guard

Do not judge P3 as failed because it is not the complete 23 second reference. Judge whether the 15 second structure is clear and technically verified.
