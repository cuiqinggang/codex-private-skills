# Do Not Auto Optimize

Never automatically optimize or delete:

1. User hard rules
2. Safety boundaries
3. Failure reviews
4. Aesthetic preferences
5. High-risk system operations
6. Long-task acceptance workflows that truly passed
7. Permission and deletion rules
8. Entry trigger conditions
9. Version dependencies
10. Judgments personally confirmed by the user

These items may be flagged for human review, but cannot be auto-merged or auto-deleted.
