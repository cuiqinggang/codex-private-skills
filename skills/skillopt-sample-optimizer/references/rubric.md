# SkillOpt Scoring Rubric

Total: 100 points.

| Dimension | Points | Meaning |
|---|---:|---|
| Entry clarity | 10 | Trigger and first actions are clear. |
| Execution steps | 15 | Workflow is concrete and ordered. |
| Rule simplicity | 10 | Rules are concise without losing meaning. |
| Hard rule retention | 15 | User safety and acceptance rules remain intact. |
| Acceptance executable | 15 | PASS/FAIL can be checked from evidence. |
| Visual evidence | 10 | UI/media/dashboard tasks require visual proof when relevant. |
| Rollback and safety | 10 | Dangerous actions stop; recovery path is explicit. |
| Dedupe and no conflict | 10 | Duplicate or conflicting rules are reduced. |
| Long-term reuse | 5 | Future Codex runs can find and reuse the lesson. |

Rejected edits should include changes that are shorter but unsafe, broader but vague, or faster but destructive.
