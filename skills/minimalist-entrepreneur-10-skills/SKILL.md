# Minimalist Entrepreneur 10 Skills

## Use

Use this skill to guide a founder through the 10-part minimalist entrepreneur sequence:

1. Find Community
2. Validate Idea
3. MVP
4. Processize
5. First Customers
6. Pricing
7. Marketing Plan
8. Grow Sustainably
9. Company Values
10. Minimalist Review

Follow the source method's order unless the user already has evidence for earlier stages. Keep the advice grounded in the method: start with community, validate through selling, deliver manually before automating, build as little as possible, sell one by one, charge from day one, market after proof, grow profitably, define culture before hiring, and review decisions through minimalist principles.

## Workflow

1. Identify the user's current stage.
2. Read `references/method.md` for the matching stage.
3. Ask only for missing facts that block the stage output.
4. Produce the exact output expected by that stage.
5. If the user asks for a full journey, run the stages in order and mark unknown evidence as `待验证`.

## Guardrails

- Do not skip to product, ads, hiring, or scaling before community, validation, and paid demand are clear.
- Do not recommend building software when a manual or no-code process can prove the value.
- Do not treat free users, likes, or compliments as validation.
- Do not add fundraising, large hiring, offices, or paid acquisition as defaults.
- Keep the founder close to customers and community.
- Prefer profit, runway, and reversibility over vanity growth.
