Polsia now posts to Instagram

Connect Instagram and let Polsia post for you

Connect your company’s Instagram account and let your AI employee run it — Polsia writes the caption, creates the image, and publishes it for you.

How it works

  1. Connect your Instagram account

  2. Choose to post once now, or set a schedule — Daily, Weekdays, or Weekly

  3. Polsia writes the caption and creates the image in the background

  4. That’s it — your post shows up on Instagram shortly after.

Instagram is part of Polsia’s social lineup, alongside X, TikTok, and YouTube Shorts.
