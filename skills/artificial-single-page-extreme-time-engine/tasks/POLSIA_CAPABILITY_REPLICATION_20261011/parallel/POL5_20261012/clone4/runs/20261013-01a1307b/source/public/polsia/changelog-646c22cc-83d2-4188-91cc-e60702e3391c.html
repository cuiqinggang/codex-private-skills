Polsia now posts to Facebook

Connect your Facebook Page and let your AI employee run it — Polsia writes each post from your company’s context and publishes it straight to your Page.

First, you’ll need a Facebook Page you manage — Polsia posts to a Page, not a personal profile (and only the company owner can connect it). Connecting takes about a minute and doesn’t post anything.

• Once or on a schedule — post now, or set Daily / Weekdays / Weekly
• Text, image, or video — pick the format in Post → Advanced; Polsia generates the media (or reuses one from your Media Library)
• Standing instructions to keep every post on-brand — draft them from chat
