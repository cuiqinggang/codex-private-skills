Polsia now posts to Pinterest

Connect Pinterest and let Polsia post for you

Connect your Pinterest account and let your AI employee run it — Polsia creates and publishes public Pins from your company's context, straight to your board.

How it works

  1. Connect your Pinterest account

  2. Select which board you want Polsia to post to

  3. Choose a format — image or video, generated by Polsia or pulled from your Media Library

  4. Set your schedule — post once now, or automatically Daily, Weekdays, or Weekly

That's it — Polsia writes the Pin and publishes it for you from there.
