# Keep List Rationale

The old package contained useful material, but most of it was not suitable for a runtime skill package.

## Why These Materials Stay

- `SKILL.md`: rewritten as a light entry and boundary file.
- `README.md`: explains candidate purpose and exclusions.
- `MANIFEST.json`: records provenance and replacement review requirement.
- `rules/`: preserves hard boundaries and route-separation rules.
- `templates/`: provides lightweight task entry patterns.
- `references/`: keeps lessons, knowledge graph notes, native route notes, and template extraction notes.
- `archive-index/`: points to old materials without copying them into the core.

## Why Most Old Assets Do Not Stay

The old assets are too large, too project-specific, and too tied to the failed Dashboard route. They should remain in archive or source packages, not in the active C-drive skill core.
