## Online Speed Interpretation

50-80 tok/s online reports can be real but are usually not comparable to this machine because they use RTX 3090/4090/5090, Linux/RunPod, Q4_K_M, special forks, DFlash/TurboQuant, or MoE models. Do not compare prompt processing TPS to generation TPS.

## Final Decision

Keep 27B MTP as a high-quality local option. Keep 14B Q5 as a lighter daily local fallback. Do not keep failed draft models or failed fork downloads.

For Kilo, use 24K context + q4 KV. For direct fast chat, use 4K context + f16 KV.
