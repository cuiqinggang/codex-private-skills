# Polsia Localization To Local AI Company System

## Role Split

GPT Web:

- External market reasoning.
- Offer and positioning drafts.
- Competitor/page interpretation.
- Chinese private-traffic script drafts.

Codex:

- Creates and updates `H:\AICompany`.
- Builds local apps, landing pages, dashboards, reports, and ledgers.
- Runs verification and writes evidence.
- Packages exports and skill updates.

Hermes:

- Maintains long-running local operations memory.
- Summarizes daily task state.
- Watches for blocked items and human-review needs.
- Bridges local assistant workflows where already configured.

OpenClaw / Xiaolongxia:

- Handles visible-browser or local UI workflows that require guarded observation.
- Works best for private-traffic admin flows after explicit approval.
- Must not auto-send messages, payments, or ads.

ComfyUI:

- Generates promotional images, resource-pack covers, posters, and short visual assets.
- Outputs should be saved under company assets and reviewed before publishing.

## Human Approval Required

- Real payment, card, bank, Stripe, KYC, subscription.
- Sending email, WeChat, SMS, DM, or support replies.
- Launching ads or spending budget.
- Publishing public posts under a real account.
- Refund, contract, legal, medical, financial, or tax commitments.
- Account deletion, irreversible data deletion, or public release.

## Never Auto-Execute

- Hidden API scraping.
- Private prompt extraction.
- Payment or payout setup.
- Cold outreach sending.
- Ad buying.
- Customer support promises.
- Brand impersonation or copied page/brand assets.
