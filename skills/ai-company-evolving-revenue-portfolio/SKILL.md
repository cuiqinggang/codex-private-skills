# AI Company Evolving Revenue Portfolio

Use this skill when the user wants to turn a small company, solo business, teaching group, community operation, consulting workflow, or product experiment into an AI-readable and AI-executable revenue system.

## Core Judgment

Treat an AI-native company as a system, not as a traditional company with AI assistants attached.

The company should be readable by AI, executable by AI, monitored by AI, and updated through repeatable review loops. For capital-scarce founders, avoid copying traditional headcount, hierarchy, meetings, and management cost. Build a context asset library, run multiple low-cost revenue paths, and let agents generate, filter, replay, and scale what works.

## Operating Rules

- Prefer context assets, customer assets, workflow assets, and replayable success paths over one-off code.
- Treat code as replaceable implementation material; preserve durable business context and decision records.
- Convert customer conversations, meeting notes, offers, tasks, product notes, code decisions, and retrospectives into AI-readable files.
- Test several small revenue paths instead of betting all resources on one path.
- Keep humans responsible for direction, authorization, exception escalation, and the final real-world step.
- Use AI-to-AI monitoring only when logs, metrics, prompts, decisions, and escalation rules remain auditable.
- Start with small systems that can produce revenue, be replayed, and be copied before aiming for large-scale automation.

## Workflow

1. Define the revenue objective in one sentence.
2. Inventory existing context assets: customers, offers, transcripts, notes, code, workflows, prompts, lessons, and failures.
3. Convert useful material into structured cards using `templates/context-asset-card.md`.
4. Create 3-7 low-cost revenue path candidates using `templates/revenue-path-card.md`.
5. Score each path by customer pain, speed to test, cost, proof strength, distribution access, repeatability, and risk.
6. Select one primary path and two backup paths for the next short cycle.
7. Assign agent roles: researcher, offer writer, customer responder, workflow executor, monitor, and reviewer.
8. Define human escalation rules before automation runs.
9. Run the smallest test that can produce real feedback or revenue.
10. Record results using `templates/replay-review-template.md`.
11. Promote repeatable wins into the success path library.
12. Retire weak paths quickly and update the context asset library.

## Polsia / Ben Cera One-Person AI Company Reality Sample

Use the Polsia material as a capability distillation sample, not as a copy target. Do not copy Polsia branding, logo, page wording, private prompts, private APIs, dashboard design, or payment mechanics.

The 2026-05-26 observation captured public/free evidence for homepage positioning, login boundary, live operations page, Terms, Product Hunt feedback, pricing, credits, revenue share, ad platform fee, IP ownership, export claims, and localization risks. Search keywords: Polsia, Ben Cera, one-person company, AI company, revenue path, 一人公司, AI公司, 收益路径.

Key conclusions:

- Free/public evidence is enough to reproduce the safe local primitives: idea intake, company workspace, daily task cycle, status report, data export, and human approval gates.
- Paid/account access is still needed to confirm the actual dashboard, credit meter, export UI, cancellation flow, and real pricing wall.
- The 20% model looks closer to gross customer revenue/payment share than net profit share.
- Ads add a separate 20% platform fee on daily ad budget and should not be automated locally.
- Local Chinese private-traffic reproduction should start with AI learning materials, family education, free resource packs, free teaching, and group Q&A.
- Small paid trial is not recommended until the only remaining goal is dashboard/credit/export verification.

## Completion Criteria

A cycle is complete only when it has:

- a written context asset update,
- at least one tested revenue path,
- observable evidence such as customer response, payment, traffic, usage, or rejection,
- a replay note explaining what can be copied,
- a clear decision to scale, revise, or stop.
