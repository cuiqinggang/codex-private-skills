# Polsia To Chinese Private Traffic MVP

## Path

First localization path:

```text
AI learning material / family education / free resource pack / free teaching / group Q&A
```

## Target Users

- Chinese parents who want children to learn AI safely.
- Adults who want a beginner-friendly AI study pack.
- Teachers/community operators who need reusable AI lesson materials.
- Private-traffic group owners who need daily Q&A and content prompts.

## Pain

- AI tools are fragmented and confusing.
- Parents do not know what is safe or useful for children.
- Many free resources are scattered, outdated, or too technical.
- Group operators cannot answer repeated beginner questions efficiently.

## Offer

Free entry offer:

- AI learning material pack.
- Parent-friendly AI tool guide.
- 7-day beginner task list.
- Free group Q&A for common questions.
- Optional paid upgrade later: guided cohort, templates, or customized family/teacher plan.

## Acquisition Channels

- WeChat private group.
- WeChat Moments/manual friend sharing.
- Xiaohongshu notes.
- Douyin short explainers.
- Existing learning/community contacts.
- Offline parent/teacher introductions.

## Group Q&A Flow

1. Human approves group topic and rules.
2. Codex prepares daily Q&A knowledge base.
3. Hermes summarizes repeated questions.
4. GPT drafts answer variants.
5. Human reviews sensitive answers.
6. OpenClaw/Xiaolongxia may assist visible UI operations only after approval.
7. No auto-send for external messages in Phase 1.

## OpenClaw / Xiaolongxia Position

Use for:

- Observing visible private-traffic admin pages.
- Preparing drafts in visible fields.
- Capturing proof screenshots.

Do not use for:

- Auto-sending group messages.
- Adding strangers.
- Mass outreach.
- Payment collection.

## ComfyUI Asset Position

Save assets under:

```text
H:\AICompany\companies\<company_id>\assets\comfyui\
```

Asset types:

- Free pack cover.
- WeChat poster.
- Xiaohongshu cover.
- Course card.
- Simple explainer images.

All generated materials require human approval before publishing.

## Manual Approval Points

- Claims about children, education outcomes, safety, or health.
- Any paid offer.
- Any external post.
- Any group send.
- Any collection of personal data.
- Any refund or service promise.

## 7-Day MVP

Day 1:

- Define target parent/learner persona.
- Create free resource-pack outline.

Day 2:

- Produce first version of AI learning pack.
- Generate poster draft with ComfyUI.

Day 3:

- Draft WeChat/Xiaohongshu/Douyin messages.
- Human approves one channel.

Day 4:

- Open small private group manually.
- Publish approved free offer.

Day 5:

- Collect top 20 questions.
- Build Q&A knowledge base.

Day 6:

- Run one assisted group Q&A session with human review.
- Record questions, objections, and conversion signals.

Day 7:

- Write status report.
- Decide continue/revise/kill.

## Success Criteria

- 20+ real people reached.
- 5+ people request the free pack.
- 3+ meaningful questions in group.
- At least 1 person asks for deeper help or a paid path.
- No compliance/safety incident.

## Failure Criteria

- No one requests the pack.
- Questions are generic and not painful.
- Human review burden is too high.
- Content requires risky claims.
- Acquisition depends on spam or mass outreach.
