# Polsia User Feedback And Failures

## Positive Feedback Observed

- Product Hunt overview and comments show strong interest in the "AI company" concept.
- A maker reply claims the system has real companies, web apps, databases, email addresses, and marketing campaigns running autonomously.
- A support-related comment says AI responses were good enough in most cases to consider replacing support, while still allowing humans to jump in.

## Negative / Concern Feedback Observed

Control:

- Users questioned whether founders can stay in control before the system goes too far.
- Maker reply says control is a key concern and that dashboard visibility, data export, and guardrails are intended to address it.

Pricing / credits:

- A user reported that after project setup, continuing required payment.
- The same user reported $49/month, limited included credits, and a cold outreach task costing 10 credits.
- The user questioned whether the value made sense compared with using Claude directly.

AI slop / realism:

- A user questioned whether public company metrics and future-looking projections were real.
- Maker reply said companies are real early-stage experiments, while some statistics are projections and need clearer grounding.

Refund:

- Maker reply offered a refund path by contacting support in response to dissatisfaction.

Data export / ownership:

- Maker reply says users own data and can export anytime.
- This still needs direct dashboard verification.

## Common Failure Classes

Product bugs:

- Generated tasks may be vague or over-costed.
- Outreach task cost may feel disproportionate.
- Dashboard/onboarding may under-explain credits.
- AI support may be wrong in edge cases or commit to meetings.
- Public metrics can be mistaken for actual realized revenue if projection labeling is unclear.

Business model risks:

- Subscription plus task credits plus 20% revenue share may feel expensive.
- Gross revenue share is dangerous for low-margin businesses.
- Ads add a second 20% fee layer on budget.
- Cold outreach and ad automation can damage reputation if not reviewed.
- Stripe/KYC assumptions do not localize cleanly to China.

## Issues Not Confirmed In Public Evidence

The public evidence reviewed did not directly confirm:

- Slow customer support as a recurring pattern.
- Large-scale task failure rates.
- Real no-revenue outcomes after paid usage.
- Actual export failure.
- Actual cancellation difficulty.

Treat those as follow-up questions for a paid/free-account trial, not confirmed facts.

## Borrowable Lessons

- Show every task, decision, and output in a dashboard.
- Make costs visible before a task runs.
- Separate "AI can draft" from "AI may execute".
- Use human approval before outreach, ads, payments, refunds, or commitments.
- Label projected metrics clearly.
- Preserve exportability as a first-class trust feature.
