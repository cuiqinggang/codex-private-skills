## Page Observations

Homepage:

- Slogan: AI that runs your company while you sleep.
- Main CTA: Get Started.
- Navigation/entries: Sign In, live banner, About, Terms, Privacy, contact email.
- Public claims: plans roadmap, ships code, runs ads, replies to customers, closes deals, posts tweets.
- Free cue: "No credit card required - Free to start" on the lower CTA section.

Login:

- Login methods visible: Google OAuth and email magic link.
- No password field observed.
- No account was used and no email was entered.

Live:

- Public live page exposes a dashboard-like operating surface.
- Visible blocks: business metrics, active companies, task cards, company list, documents, Twitter feed, email feed, ads section entry, and "Ask Polsia anything" panel.
- Evidence structure: Polsia demonstrates autonomy through running task cards, elapsed timers, aggregate metrics, recent companies, recent emails, and public examples of active work.

Terms:

- Terms confirm ad-spend authorization, daily charges, Stripe processing, Stripe Connect withdrawals, refund/chargeback deduction, ownership, AI content responsibility, and platform ownership.

Product Hunt:

- Product description: AI that runs your company while you sleep.
- Public copy says Polsia autonomously runs planning, coding, marketing, and operations.
- Comments contain user concerns about control, credits, pricing, AI slop, realism, data export, and revenue share.

Blog:

- The Devin alternatives article positions Polsia as broader than coding agents: product planning, development, marketing, and customer operations.
- It explicitly argues the problem is building a business, not only code.

## Page Structure Summary

Polsia presents itself as an AI operating company, not a coding tool. Public pages use four proof surfaces:

- Promise: homepage claims one AI can run company roles.
- Gate: login creates a normal account boundary.
- Proof theater / live telemetry: public live page exposes task streams and aggregate business metrics.
- Risk/legal layer: terms define payments, ads, refunds, ownership, and AI-content responsibility.
