## Absorbable Mechanisms

1. Demand signal: comment-section questions are early demand evidence.
2. Hotspot validation: check X/Twitter and related communities for topic heat.
3. Product wedge: simplify a new AI platform feature into a one-click public web product.
4. Build route: use Codex or another top coding agent to ship a small web product quickly.
5. Model judgment: top-tier model quality matters more than cheapest model choice when output quality is the product.
6. Payment readiness: complete product clarity, privacy policy, terms, and refund pages before applying for payment processors.
7. Payment options for China-based solo builders: Paddle, Creem, Stripe; Stripe may require compatible region/bank verification.
8. Distribution tactic: comment under high-intent, high-traffic posts instead of only posting from a cold account.
9. Meme/newsjacking tactic: connect the product to timely public figures or hot events to increase click curiosity.
10. Success threshold: first payment and 4-5 orders are enough to prove signal for a water-test project.

## Reusable SOP

1. Watch for new AI platform features or viral examples.
2. Capture repeated audience questions as demand candidates.
3. Search X/Twitter/Reddit/Product Hunt/TikTok/Xiaohongshu for heat and comparable products.
4. Define a tiny transformation product: one input -> one desirable output.
5. Build the smallest web version with Codex.
6. Test output quality with top available models before optimizing cost.
7. Add legal/payment readiness pages: privacy, terms, refund, product description.
8. Connect payment through the lowest-friction viable platform.
9. Publish and distribute by commenting under relevant high-traffic posts.
10. Track first payment, orders, conversion source, and failure reasons.
11. Decide whether to continue only after confirming signal, not excitement.

## Failure Modes

- Applying for payment before product/legal pages are complete.
- Using a cheaper model when product value depends on output quality.
- Posting only from a cold account instead of entering existing attention streams.
- Treating a water-test as a full company before first-payment evidence.
