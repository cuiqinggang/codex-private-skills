## Core Mechanism

This is not an "AI builds random websites" route. It is a lead-quality and delivery-quality route:

1. Pick a fragmented local-service niche.
2. Use map/search platforms to find businesses with visible demand and weak digital presence.
3. Score leads by commercial likelihood, not just technical defects.
4. Produce a relevant demo or diagnosis.
5. Contact with a specific problem and specific improvement, not generic "do you need a website" copy.
6. Sell a fixed-scope deployed outcome.
7. Convert repeated work into templates, agents, checklists, and deployment recipes.

## Reusable SOP

1. Choose a niche where a better website can plausibly increase bookings, trust, calls, visits, or orders.
2. Pick one geography small enough to manually understand.
3. Pull candidate businesses from Google Map or another public directory.
4. Screen for visible pain:
   - no website
   - broken mobile page
   - outdated design
   - no clear reservation/contact/order path
   - weak photos/menu/service presentation
   - bad third-party listing dependency
5. Score business viability:
   - can contact the owner or manager
   - business appears active
   - likely has budget
   - website improvement would make the owner proud
   - likely return from trust/bookings/orders is believable
6. Create a compact diagnosis and, when worthwhile, a tiny demo.
7. Contact with a specific observation and offer.
8. Deliver only a real deployed site with clear scope, revisions, and handoff.
9. Track time spent, response rate, close rate, delivery time, revisions, and realized price.
10. Convert repeated delivery steps into reusable templates and agents.

## Lead Scoring Checklist

Use this before spending demo time:

| Gate | Pass Signal | Fail Signal |
|---|---|---|
| Demand | Searches, reviews, foot traffic, booking/order intent | No clear customer acquisition path |
| Pain | Site missing, broken, outdated, or mobile-hostile | Existing site already good enough |
| Contact | Owner/manager/email/phone reachable | No realistic contact route |
| Budget | Business seems stable enough to pay GBP 600-1500 | Micro operation with no likely budget |
| Pride | Owner would benefit reputationally from a better site | Owner likely does not care |
| Specificity | Outreach names a concrete defect and benefit | Generic cold email copy |
| Delivery | Can ship within known templates and scope | Requires custom platform, risky integrations, or unclear scope |

## Offer Template

Position the offer as a business repair, not a tech trick:

```text
I noticed your current website/listing is losing mobile visitors at the moment of intent.
I can deliver a clean, mobile-ready, editable website for your business with [menu/service/photos/contact/reservation/order] included.
The scope is fixed, deployed, and handed over after revisions. The goal is not a fancy redesign; it is to stop losing high-intent local search traffic.
```

## Failure Modes

- Treating Google Map scraping as the business, while ignoring qualification and delivery.
- Sending long, generic cold emails that sound like every other website vendor.
- Building demos for every lead instead of only qualified leads.
- Selling a screenshot or mock instead of a deployed site.
- Underpricing early custom work without measuring hours and revision load.
- Over-automating before the human buying-signal checklist is understood.
- Copying the exact niche without local knowledge; the route transfers better as a method than as "UK restaurants only".
